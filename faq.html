Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SwiftCV Pro.

How do I save my resume?

Simply click the "Sync to Cloud" button at the end of the form. Your data is instantly stored in our Supabase cloud database.

Can I edit my resume later?

Yes! On your Dashboard, click "Edit Details" on any saved resume to update your information.

Is it free to download as PDF?

Absolutely. Once you finish the third step of the builder, click "Download PDF" to save a copy to your device.
